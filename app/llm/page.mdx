# LLMs

Large language models are really just enormous groups of numbers. A tremendous amount of time and energy has been spent on adjusting those numbers in such a way that they can be used in [a well-known mathematical calculation](/linalg/matrix/multiplication) to produce useful text.

These are specialized topics regarding large language models.
