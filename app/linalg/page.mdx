# Linear Algebra

Linear algebra is the study of structure and change through linear transformations. It first appeared in [ancient Chinese mathematics](/linalg/fangcheng) as a technique that we know today as [Gaussian elimination](/linalg/solver/gaussian) - a step-by-step method for solving systems of linear equations by reducing them to a simpler "diagonalized" form.
