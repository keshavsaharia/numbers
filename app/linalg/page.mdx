# Linear Algebra

Linear algebra is a fundamental area of mathematics focused on the study of vectors, matrices, and linear transformations. It provides the theoretical framework for solving systems of linear equations, which is crucial in various scientific and engineering applications. 

One of the key techniques in linear algebra is [Gaussian elimination](/linalg/solver/gaussian), a technique that first appeared in [ancient Chinese mathematics](/linalg/fangcheng) and was later formalized by European mathematicians in the 18th century.
